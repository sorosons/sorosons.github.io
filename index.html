About Us

A Mobile-First Software Company from İstanbul

SoroSons (SORO SONS BİLGİ TEKNOLOJİSİ TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ) is a mobile software company founded in İstanbul. Since 2019 we have been designing and publishing applications and games used by people around the world.

Our portfolio spans games, music tools, books, navigation, and AI-powered creative apps — all built with a focus on quality, performance, and user experience.

  • Native iOS & Android development
  • Full lifecycle: design, development, publishing, support
  • 12+ apps published across the App Store and Google Play
