How It Works

1

Install ProX and grant the permissions it asks for (notification and photo library access).

2

Receive media as usual in your messaging apps — photos, videos, voice messages or statuses.

3

Open ProX to find your media saved and ready in your library, even “view once” content.

A note on fair use: ProX only works with content that reaches your own device. Please respect the privacy and rights of the people who send you content, and use the app only where permitted by law and by the terms of the messaging service concerned.
