Privacy Policy for Sorosons

Effective Date: Sep 01, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared in connection with the "Sorosons" account and its applications, including apps that provide navigation and maps features. By using our apps, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

Certain personally identifiable information may be collected and used. This includes, but is not limited to, your name, email address, interactions, and data necessary to provide navigation and mapping services. This information is not shared with unauthorized third parties and is handled with care and confidentiality.

Types of Data Collected

1. Personal Data

Information such as your name and email address, especially if you contact us for support or feedback.

2. Location Data

Our navigation and maps applications access and use your device's location (both foreground and background) to provide real-time navigation, route planning, location-based search, and personalized map features.

  • Location data may be collected continuously while the app is running, including when the app is in the background, to enable accurate navigation.
  • This data is used only for providing app features and improving functionality.
  • Location information is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Device Information

Data about the device used to access this account, such as device model, operating system, and network information.

4. Usage Data

Details on how you interact with our apps, including access times, navigation features used, and performance statistics.

5. Log Data

In case of errors or technical issues, information such as IP address, device name, software version, and timestamps may be collected for diagnostics.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Cookies or similar technologies may be used to remember preferences and improve experience. You can control or disable cookies in your browser settings.

Use of Data

Collected data may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide navigation and maps services, including real-time guidance and route suggestions
  • To manage and maintain the account
  • To provide support or respond to inquiries
  • To improve functionality and user experience
  • To detect and resolve technical issues
  • To meet legal or regulatory requirements

Third-Party Services

The "Sorosons" account may use third-party tools or services for analytics, navigation, or system support. These services may collect certain technical data to function properly. Examples include:

  • Google Maps and related Google Services
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics
  • Other infrastructure providers used to deliver app functionality

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data, including location data. However, no digital system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. You acknowledge and accept this inherent risk.

Links to External Sites

If links to external websites are ever provided, please be aware that those sites are not covered under this policy. You are encouraged to review their individual privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a child's data is accidentally collected, it will be deleted upon discovery or upon parental request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. You are advised to review it occasionally for any changes. Updates will be posted with the latest effective date.
