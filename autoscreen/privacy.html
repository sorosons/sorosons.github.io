Privacy Policy — AutoScreen

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the AutoScreen app for iPhone and Apple CarPlay ("AutoScreen", "the app") processes, why, with whom it is shared and what choices you have. AutoScreen is published by SORO SONS BİLGİ TEKNOLOJİSİ TİCARET LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ, Turkey ("we", "us"), which is the controller of the personal data described here.

In short: AutoScreen has no user accounts. Your location is used on your device for speed camera warnings and navigation. Your screen mirroring never leaves your iPhone and car. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information we process

1.1 Location

With your permission, AutoScreen uses your device's precise location, also while the app runs in the background during a drive, to warn you about speed cameras ahead, to show your position on the map, for turn-by-turn navigation and to show your speed on the car screen.

  • Location is processed on your device. We do not keep a history of your trips.
  • To load the speed cameras of an area, the app asks our database for that map area.
  • Place search and route calculation are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit): your search text, your position and your destination are sent to Apple to return results and routes, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Your home, work and recent destinations are stored only on your device.
  • We do not use your location for advertising and do not sell it.

1.2 Speed camera reports, confirmations and comments

When you report a speed camera or a hazard, the reported position, its type, the direction of travel and the time are stored in our database and shown to all users. Reports of mobile radars, police, accidents and hazards expire automatically after a few hours. When you confirm a camera, write a comment or report a comment, we store it together with a random identifier of your app installation, so you can delete your own comments and so abuse can be blocked. These features are not linked to your name, e-mail or Apple ID.

1.3 Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment card details. The app receives the transaction information from Apple (product, purchase and expiry dates, status) to unlock Premium on your device.

1.4 Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad and prevent fraud, Google may collect your IP address, device and app information, an app-specific device identifier and your interactions with ads. AutoScreen does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are personalized we ask for your consent through Google's consent message (IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). You can refuse, and you can withdraw or change your choice at any time by contacting us, or from the privacy options in the app's Settings where they are shown. If you do not consent, you see limited, non-personalized ads.

Premium users do not see ads. Ads are never shown on the car screen. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

1.5 App measurement

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): anonymous usage statistics such as screens opened, app version, device model, country and language, to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): crash reports (device model, iOS version, the state of the app at the crash) to fix errors.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): settings of the app that we can adjust without an update.
  • Meta App Events (Meta Platforms): app installs, launches and purchase events, to measure the results of our own advertising campaigns. We do not use it to track you across apps and websites of other companies.

1.6 Notifications

If you allow notifications, a push token, your device type, language and time zone are processed by OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

1.7 Screen mirroring and videos

  • Screen mirroring sends the picture of your iPhone directly to your car display. The picture is never uploaded, stored or seen by us.
  • Videos you add from your photo library or record with the camera are copied into the app's storage on your device only.
  • Channel lists (M3U links) and websites you open are loaded directly from the addresses you enter. AutoScreen does not provide any content. Websites you open in the built-in browser, such as YouTube, apply their own privacy policies and cookies.
  • Voice search uses Apple's speech recognition; the audio may be processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. AutoScreen does not record or keep audio.
  • Voice guidance and spoken warnings are generated or played on your device.

1.8 Support

If you e-mail us, we use your e-mail address and message only to answer you.

2. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Performance of a contract: providing the app's features you use (warnings, navigation, car screen, Premium).
  • Consent: location and notification permissions (asked by iOS) and personalized advertising.
  • Legitimate interests: app security, crash analysis, preventing abuse of reports and comments, measuring and improving the app.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

4. How long we keep data

  • Reports of mobile radars, police, accidents and hazards: expire after hours; fixed speed cameras stay in the database while they exist.
  • Comments: until you or we delete them.
  • Data on your device (settings, destinations, your videos): until you delete it or the app.
  • Analytics, crash and advertising data: as set by the providers above, typically up to 14–26 months.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by the CCPA.

To use your rights, e-mail us. Because the app has no accounts, please include the approximate time and place of a report or the text of a comment so we can find it. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Your choices in the app and on your iPhone: location, notifications, camera, photos and microphone permissions in iOS Settings; advertising consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) by contacting us or from the app's privacy options where shown; removing ads with Premium.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we process.

7. Children

AutoScreen is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA) and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
